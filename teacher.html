Chapter One · The Beginning

A Newspaper as a Teacher

Before the internet became necessary in his workplace, my father learned how to write by reading newspapers.

When he moved into the company’s publicity department, he knew how to write short pieces for the company newspaper. Professional reports, however, felt like another language.

One of his teachers gave him a copy of China Aviation News. It became more than something to read—it became a model he could study each morning.
