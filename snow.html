Chapter Three · A Winter Assignment

Three Days in the Snow

The memory he recalls most clearly began when he traveled with an experimental team into the Qinling Mountains.

01

Entering the Mountains

Heavy snow had closed many roads, and the temperature was very low. He arrived to stay beside the people doing the work.

02

Listening Before Writing

He recorded what the team did and listened to their stories. A technical experiment became a story about people who believed the work mattered.

03

The Details He Kept

He wrote down the small moments: frozen faces, exhaustion, and the laughter that appeared while they worked.

04

Carrying the Story Home

After three days and three nights, he returned with notes, photographs, and a responsibility to help readers see the people behind the work.
